ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Turning public market data into useful signals.

This student project explores AI/ML models for short-term wholesale price forecasting of onions, potatoes, and pulses. The planned analysis compares model accuracy and studies the role of seasonality, arrivals, and optional weather data.
